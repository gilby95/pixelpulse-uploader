Privacy policy

PIXELPULSE Uploader · effective 9 October 2026

Who uses it

PIXELPULSE Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the PIXELPULSE YouTube channel, on his own computer, signed in with his own Google account. It has no other users.

What it accesses

  • The owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API: uploading videos, editing their titles, descriptions, playlists and privacy, and reading the channel's own statistics.
  • Public Twitch clips and their public details, to make the videos and credit the streamers.

Where it is kept

The sign-in token and the channel's statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any other server except Google's and Twitch's own APIs.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which also stops it using the stored token.
