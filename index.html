PIXELPULSE Uploader

A personal tool for one YouTube channel.

PIXELPULSE Uploader is the small program that runs the PIXELPULSE YouTube channel. It turns public Twitch clips into Shorts and compilation videos, credits the streamers in each description, and uploads them to the PIXELPULSE channel on a schedule. It also reads that channel's own YouTube Analytics so the next videos can be better.

It is used only by the channel's owner, on his own computer, with his own YouTube account. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else signs in to it.
